# Experience System — Design Principles

These govern every product decision for gauravnagarkoti.tech.

1. **Business-first, not technology-first.**
2. **Clarity over decoration.**
3. **Story over features.**
4. **Trust before persuasion.**
5. **Performance is part of the design.**
6. **Accessibility is mandatory.**
7. **Motion serves meaning.**
8. **Every page must advance the visitor toward a decision.**

> Motion exists to reinforce business value, guide attention, and build confidence—not to demonstrate technical skill.

Before adding an effect: *Does this help the visitor understand, trust, or take action?* If no, leave it out.
